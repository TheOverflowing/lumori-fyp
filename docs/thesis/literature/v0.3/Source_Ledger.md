## Original sources and figure permission

The ACL originals are downloaded from `https://aclanthology.org/<anthology-id>.pdf`. The Lost in the Middle PDF explicitly states CC BY 4.0 on its first page. Its landing page https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/ also links https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The chart is cropped, with axes and legend intact, and attributed in its thesis caption. See `../../figures/literature-middle-provenance.json` for crop coordinates, source hash and change declaration. No endorsement is implied.

The `papers/` folder also includes ALCE for checking support/precision distinctions. We do not import its headline accuracy into the Lumori results. Original wording is paraphrased; numerical values are attributed at point of use.
